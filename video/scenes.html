BDSMA MICRO PROJECT · B.E. COMPUTER · SEM VII

SkyTrace

Flight Trajectory Analysis using Big Data
SDG 9 · Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure
WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?

Google Maps traffic, but for the sky

  • Every plane sends its position every second (ADS-B), like a GPS ping
  • Joining these points gives the flight's path, called a trajectory
  • We study WHERE and WHEN the sky is crowded (spatio-temporal analytics)
  • Using big data tools: Hadoop, MapReduce, Spark, MongoDB, graph analysis
WHY DO WE NEED THIS?

Crowded skies waste fuel and time

  • Delhi and Mumbai airports are packed during the morning and evening rush
  • When the runway is full, planes circle in the air and wait, called holding
  • Planes follow bent air routes instead of straight lines
  • 15 minutes of circling ≈ 500–600 kg of extra fuel for one A320
  • SDG 9: data tells us where new runways and better routes are needed
